What we built

A complete escrow product,
not a mock-up.

Milestone escrow

Funds lock in a Solidity smart contract. Each milestone settles independently — neither party controls the money alone.

Dispute arbitration

A predefined arbitrator releases the disputed milestone — or refunds it. Their decision is recorded on-chain.

IPFS evidence

Agreements and deliverables pin to IPFS from the app. The ipfs:// CID is stored on-chain — tamper-resistant proof of what was submitted.

On-chain reputation

score = released milestones + 5 × completed jobs

Computed from real settlements — no self-written ratings.

ETH + ERC-20

Native ETH escrow, plus the standard token flow — approve → transferFrom → transfer — with a valueless testnet mUSDC.

Wallet-native app

MetaMask-connected Next.js interface: create, fund, deliver, approve, dispute, arbitrate — every role in one place.
